Reading team coverage

Each rumor in the archive is tagged with the teams it concerns, so a reporter's team split shows where their sourcing actually lives. Beat writers dominate their own team and barely register elsewhere. National reporters spread thinly across all 30.

The share column is that reporter's slice of all archived mentions for the team in the selected period, which is the quickest way to see whether a team's news flows through one person or a crowded field.
