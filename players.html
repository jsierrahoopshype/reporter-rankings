Reading player coverage

Rumors are tagged by player, so this view answers a question agents and front offices track closely: when an item about a given player is archived, whose byline is on it?

Concentration matters more than raw volume. A player whose mentions cluster around one or two reporters usually has a tight information channel. A player spread across dozens of names is being covered nationally, or is in the middle of something everyone is chasing.
