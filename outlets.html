Outlets versus bylines

Some archived rumors are credited to a publication rather than a person: a staff report, a wire item, a team broadcast. Those land here instead of on the reporter leaderboard.

Outlet totals also shift when reporters move. A byline that changes employer takes its future mentions with it, so an outlet's ranking reflects who was working there at the time each item was archived.
