What the heatmap shows

Each cell is one reporter's archived mentions in one month, shaded against the busiest cell on screen. Deadline weeks, the draft and the opening of free agency show up as vertical bands where almost everyone lights up at once.

The horizontal reading is more useful. A steady row is a reporter with a consistent beat. A row that stops is someone who changed jobs, moved off the NBA, or left the industry, and a row that starts mid-grid is a newer voice arriving.
